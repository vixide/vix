How to suggest an idea for Vix

The Ideas category is where we talk about changes and features before anyone writes code. The best ideas start with a problem, not a solution, so we can find the right fix together, even if it's a different one.

Please post one idea per discussion, so feedback on each stays in one place. Before you post, search [Ideas](https://github.com/vixide/vix/discussions/categories/ideas) and [Issues](https://github.com/vixide/vix/issues) in case someone already suggested it, and add your case there instead.

## How to suggest an idea

Copy these headings into a new discussion and fill them in:

```markdown
## Problem
What gets in your way, who it affects, and when it happens.

## Proposal
What you'd like Vix to do, in plain terms. A short list is fine.

## Alternatives
What you do today instead, other ways to solve it, and what happens
if Vix does nothing.

## Costs and trade-offs
Anything you can think of: complexity, compatibility with existing
keymaps or settings, or work to maintain.

## Open questions
What you'd like others to weigh in on.
```

Give it a title that names the problem or the outcome, such as "Hard to tell which split has focus", rather than "New feature".

## Before you ask for a feature

Vix may already do it. Press `F1` for every shortcut, or `Ctrl+P` for every command. Small, personal commands can often be a [Rhai script](https://github.com/vixide/vix/blob/main/docs/scripting/index.md) instead of a change to Vix.

## What happens next

Maintainers read new ideas and reply in the thread. An idea we agree to take on becomes an [Issue](https://github.com/vixide/vix/issues), and we'll link it from the discussion. A suggestion isn't a commitment to build it, and some ideas will be a "not for now". When that happens, we'll say why.
